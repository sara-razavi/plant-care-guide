Basic Tips

  • Check the soil with your finger before watering. If the top is still wet, wait a few more days.
  • Too much water kills more house plants than too little.
  • Use pots with drainage holes so the roots don't sit in water.
  • "Bright indirect light" means near a window, but not in the direct sun.
  • Most plants grow slower in winter so they need less water.
  • Wipe dusty leaves with a wet cloth, the dust blocks light.
